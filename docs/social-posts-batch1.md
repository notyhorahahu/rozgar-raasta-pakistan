# Rozgar Raasta Pakistan — Social Posts, Batch 1

**Platforms:** Facebook and Pinterest only. **Instagram is NOT used for this project.**
**Honesty rules (apply to every post):** Attractive, truthful headlines that match what the article actually delivers. No "earn lots of money", no guaranteed income, no fake salaries, no fake government logos, no invented deadlines or testimonials. Each post links to its real article URL. Graphic: edit the named template in `social/graphics/` (brand colours navy `#0f2440`, blue `#2563eb`, white, slate `#334155`; keep the footer with the site name + URL), export at the stated size, then post.

**How to use:** One FB post + one Pinterest Pin per article. Do not post the same text twice — the FB hook and the Pin description are written differently on purpose. Post at most 1–2 items per article per day, spread across the week.
