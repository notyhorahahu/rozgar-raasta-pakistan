Write a CV That Gets Interviews

Five steps, in order. Keep it to one page. Tailor it to each job.

1

Start with your best information

Name, phone, email, city — at the top. No photo, no CNIC, no father's name needed.

2

Write a 2–3 line summary

Who you are, your strongest skill, and the job you want. No long paragraphs.

3

List education, newest first

Degree, institution, year. Add relevant courses only.

4

Show experience with results

What you did and what changed. Use numbers where you honestly can.

5

Proofread, then send as PDF

Check spelling twice. Save as PDF so the format never breaks.
